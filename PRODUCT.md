product

## Users & Purpose

TradeEdge is a crypto trading signal bot dashboard. The primary users are traders who want to monitor their trading signals, analyze performance, and backtest strategies.

The dashboard serves as the central command center for:
- Monitoring live/open trades in real-time
- Reviewing trade history and outcomes
- Analyzing performance metrics (win rate, RR, direction, strength)
- Running backtests on historical data

## Brand Personality

Professional, precise, data-driven. The interface should feel like a premium trading terminal - clean, dark, and focused on data clarity. Think Bloomberg Terminal meets modern SaaS dashboard.

## Anti-references

- No bright, playful colors (this is a serious trading tool)
- No cluttered, information-dense layouts without hierarchy
- No light theme (traders prefer dark mode for long sessions)
- No generic bootstrap-style components

## Accessibility

- WCAG AA minimum
- Support for reduced motion
- Color should not be the only indicator of status (use icons + text)
